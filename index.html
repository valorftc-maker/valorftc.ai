§ 03 — The Language

One language for how money moves.

VAL is a declarative language built for finance and nothing else. Amounts carry their currency in the type system. Compliance guards are syntax, not comments — a flow that violates policy fails to compile, not to clear. Every policy is version-controlled, code-reviewed, and dry-run against production-shaped data before it touches a dollar. When it compiles, it becomes a deterministic execution plan: same inputs, same outcome, every time. Your finance logic stops living in spreadsheets, tickets, and tribal memory. It lives in a repo.

§ 04 — Intelligence

AI that engineers, never improvises.

Valor's models draft VAL from plain-English intent, simulate proposed policies against months of your actual flow history, and watch live traffic for patterns no threshold would catch. Then they stop. The AI proposes; the deterministic runtime disposes. Every suggestion arrives as code you can read, diff, test, and reject — and nothing ships without a human approval recorded in the ledger. No model moves money. That is the point of the architecture, not a limitation of it.

§ 05 — Custody & Control

Built like infrastructure, guarded like a vault.

Valor is built to SOC 2 standards from the first commit: encryption in transit and at rest, hardware-backed key management, least-privilege access by default, and an append-only audit log signed at every state change. Because compliance rules are enforced at the language level, the unsafe path isn't discouraged — it's unrepresentable. Designed so customer funds sit with regulated, FDIC-insured partner banks — and every control we claim is one you can inspect.

SOC 2 standards from the first commit

Controls are designed in, not bolted on — least-privilege access by default, every claim inspectable.

Hardware-backed keys, encrypted end to end

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with key material held in hardware — never in application code.

Append-only, signed audit log

Every state change is signed as it happens. History can be extended, never edited.

Unsafe paths are unrepresentable

Compliance is enforced at the language level, and custody is designed around regulated, FDIC-insured partner banks.
