The book

eleven production strategies · five disciplines

Uncorrelated edges, combined into one book at a chosen level of risk. No single strategy is the firm; the process that produces them is.

The method

most of the work is the machinery, not the signal

Most of the work in quant trading is not finding a signal. It is building machinery honest enough to tell you the signal isn't real — and then having the discipline to believe it.

Every idea runs the same gauntlet before a dollar moves. Most die here, and the log of what died is itself an asset — six months on, the same idea comes back and the log saves the week.

Gate 01

Is it real?

Multiple-testing corrected. The net-Sharpe confidence interval must exclude zero after paying for every trial in the search. A single flattering backtest is not evidence.

Gate 02

What did it cost?

Gross and net always travel together. Realistic slippage, turnover, and financing are charged before anything ships. If cost eats the edge, it was never edge.

Gate 03

Would it survive?

Walk-forward, strictly out of sample. Parameters are chosen only on data the test never saw. The holdout is opened once — tuning against it is not allowed, by rule and in code.

Risk & governance

guarded like it's someone else's money — because it will be

Written before there's a position, on purpose. Mid-drawdown you're a different decision-maker with worse judgment; the rules exist to bind that person. Live routing is blocked in code until the gates are met — not by convention.
