First time?
  1. Ask an admin to create your account — you'll set your own password on the spot, and it is never sent by email.
  2. Add the authenticator secret to 1Password, Authy, or Google Authenticator, and store your recovery codes somewhere safe. They are shown once.
  3. Connect to the VPN, then open the desk and sign in with your username, password, and the six-digit code.

Five wrong attempts lock an account for fifteen minutes. Sessions end after 1 hour idle or 12 hours absolute. Every sign-in, failure, and permission denial is written to an append-only audit log. Access to the desk grants research and paper-trading only — live routing remains blocked in code, for everyone, regardless of role.
